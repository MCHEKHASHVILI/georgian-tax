## Questions for the owner

A question stays here until it is answered. Items that depend on it are blocked until then.

- 2026-11-12 — **Excise credit on exports (189.1).** 189.1 gives an exporter of excise goods the credit (or refund) of the excise on the raw materials, "but not more than the excise calculated on the excise goods made from them". The export itself is exempt with credit (194.6.ა), so no excise is calculated on it. Options: (a) cap the credit at the excise the exported goods would bear at the rate in force (quantity × rate), and report that creditable amount on the export's `ExemptWithCredit`; (b) leave the export credit to the caller, as now. **Recommendation: (a)** — it is the only reading under which the export credit in 189.1 does anything. Blocks the export half of "Next" item 1's 189.

   - 177: VAT apportionment;

2. **Per-country ledger.** Found while doing the per-country tables: `Ledger` (and georgian-tax's `YearToDate`) is still one global binding, so a second country's package would record into, and read year-to-date totals from, Georgia's ledger. Key it by country the way `CountryTables` does.
3. **API: a draft's diff.** Found while doing the JSON API: `GET drafts/{id}` shows the payload, but not the value in force it would close. A reviewer approving an amendment wants both side by side (`current`: the row in force on the draft's `from`). Small; after the MVP.
4. **Gaps found in 82.2 and 98⁴** (2026-11-05), each small, with tests citing the article:
   - 82.2.ა.ვ is not modelled: a high-mountain resident with three or more children under 18 pays no income tax on salary from a budget organization there; with one or two, the tax on the first 3,000 GEL of it a year is halved.
   - 98⁴.2: when last year's costs exceeded revenue, the representation limit is 1% of the costs. `RepresentationOverLimit` uses revenue only (its docblock says otherwise); `CompanyBooks` needs last year's costs.
5. **Postal tobacco and alcohol under 199.დ.ვ** (found 2026-11-06). Besides chapters 28–97 up to 300 GEL, 199.დ.ვ relieves from import tax 200 cigarettes (or the other tobacco quantities) and 4 litres of alcohol by post, with no value limit in the text. `ImportTax\Stage\ApplyExemptions` covers only chapters 28–97 for `PostalSmall`, so a bottle of whisky by post pays import tax. Cover chapters 22 and 24 for it without the 300 GEL cap, quantities for the caller, with a test.
6. **Motorcycles: what row 4¹ of 188.1 leaves out** (found 2026-11-07). The row reads "8711 (გარდა 8711 სასაქონლო პოზიციით გათვალისწინებული ეტლისა)": heading 8711 except the ეტლი under it. `Motorcycles` taxes all of 8711. Find the HS subheading the exclusion means and leave it out, with a test.
7. **Check the Code for amendments.** Compare the cited articles with the latest consolidated text on matsne.gov.ge, and record each change through the actions (in both the files and the seeder), citing the amending law.
8. **202.2.ბ: state enterprises keep historic cost** (found 2026-11-08). 202.2 lists two cases the 202.1.ა–დ revaluation does not reach: audited revaluation (ა, modelled as `revaluedWithAudit`) and state enterprises the Government names (ბ, not modelled). `CompanyProperty` triples a listed state enterprise's pre-2000 real estate. Add the flag (which enterprises are listed is for the caller), with a test.
9. **Reports spanning two countries** (found 2026-11-09). An engine with two countries' taxes keys a report's outcomes by tax name only, so two countries' `vat` overwrite each other, and `totalTax()` adds their amounts in one currency (the first tax's), throwing `CurrencyMismatch` when they differ. Stored assessments now name each outcome's country, so the data can tell them apart; the report cannot yet. Key outcomes by country and tax (or keep one report per country) and total per currency. Matters only once a second country's package exists.
10. **Temporary admission: what the Code leaves to the Customs Code** (found 2026-11-11). The engine charges import tax under 197.4–5 and nothing else: VAT and excise in the Tax Code charge import (160², 183.ბ), which 8.47–48 separate from temporary admission. Partial relief from VAT on temporary admission, if any, is in the Customs Code, which `docs/law` does not hold. If the owner wants it, add the Customs Code's articles to `docs/law` first. Also left to the caller: whether the goods qualify for temporary admission at all, and paying a month's share (the outcome is the total, due on the first instalment's day).
11. **Day counts and non-working days** (found 2026-11-14). The figures moved into tables are amounts and rates; the Code's periods are still constants in code — 180 days in 189.8 and 198.3 (`ReexportVehicle::DAYS`), 6 months in 82.1.ვ.ბ (`HeldAssetGain`). And 3.6 moves a last day that falls on a non-working day to the next working day, which no deadline here applies (`Deadline`, `ReexportVehicle::lastDay()`): that needs Georgia's public holidays (the Labour Code's list), which `docs/law` does not hold. Small each; ask the owner before adding a holiday calendar.
