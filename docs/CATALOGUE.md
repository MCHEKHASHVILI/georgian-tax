## Withheld at source (Art. 154.1)

## Special regimes (Art. 83–95⁶)

| Regime | Effect | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Micro business (≤ 30,000 GEL a year, no employees) | No income tax on business income; payers don't withhold | 84, 86, 94.3 |
| Small business (individual entrepreneur) | 1% of Georgian-source turnover, excluding salary; 3% from the month turnover exceeds 500,000 GEL (700,000 GEL for wine or agro-tourism) | 90.1–90.3 |
| Fixed tax | Government-set amount per object (1–2,000 GEL), or 3% of income from the fixed-tax activity; other income taxed normally | 95³, 95⁵ |
